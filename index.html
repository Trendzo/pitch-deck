01  The problem · Consumers

Online fashion still asks you to buy blind.

01
Fit is a guess Nobody touches the fabric or checks the fit before paying.
02
Returns take days Money sits frozen on both sides while a reverse pickup happens.
03
Photos lie Studio shots and screen colors rarely match what shows up at the door.
04
Trust is the real tax Fashion's high return rates are a trust problem, not a shipping problem.
02  The problem · Retailers

The stock exists. The storefront doesn't.

01
No storefront Millions of single-store retailers hold stock with no way to list it online.
02
Photography is a wall Studio-grade product shots are expensive and slow, so most never go digital.
03
Marketplaces aren't built for them Big platforms want full catalogs and dedicated ops, not a 200-SKU shop.
04
Cash flow bleeds Long payout cycles and high commissions make going online a net loss for a small store.
03  The problem · Aggregators

Every platform solved half the problem.

01
Quick commerce solved speed, not certainty Blinkit, Zepto and Instamart proved fast delivery works, but speed alone can't fix fit.
02
Everyone defaults to warehouses The instinct is always owned inventory in a warehouse, capital-heavy and slow to open, while the stock already sits on shelves five minutes from the buyer.
03
Inventory nobody can trust Even platforms that plug into local stores show stock that's already sold, so orders fail after the customer has paid.
04
Nobody owns the "try" moment Every platform ships a box and hopes it fits. None put a person at the door to close the sale live.
04  How we work · Retailer diagnostic

We asked retailers four questions before we built anything.

01
No real social presence 55% have no active social media account promoting the store.
02
Not selling online anywhere 82% have no listing on any platform and no website of their own.
03
Billing that doesn't talk to stock 70% run billing that isn't connected to their shelf inventory at all.
04
Tried, not bought, every single day 65% of what gets tried on in a day gets refolded and put back, never sold.

11  Trust and compliance

The compliance is the moat.

01
Invoices are never edited Credit notes and supplementary invoices only, exactly as GST law requires.
02
KYC before a single rupee moves GSTIN portal check, PAN validation, bank penny-drop, annual re-verification.
03
Nothing is silently overwritten Every order, return, payout, stock move and price edit is a permanent event.
04
Humans decide, not algorithms A documented strike ladder with an Ops call before any suspension.
